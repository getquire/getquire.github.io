Terms of service

Plain language, because a term you can't understand isn't one you agreed to.

Last updated 12 August 2026 · Quire Labs Ltd.

The short version

  • You own your content. We own the software. Neither of us takes the other's.
  • Pay monthly or annually; cancel any time and it stops at the end of the period.
  • The output is a draft, not advice. A human is accountable for what gets sent.
  • If we go down badly, you get credits; if we breach the contract, you can leave.

1. The agreement

These terms apply between Quire Labs Ltd. and the organization on the account. If you're signing on behalf of a company, you're confirming you can. Enterprise customers usually sign a negotiated order form, which takes precedence over anything here that conflicts with it.

2. Your account

You're responsible for who you let in and what they do. Keep credentials secure, use SSO if you have it, and tell us at security@quire.ai if you think an account is compromised. Workspace admins can see usage metadata for their members — including who asked how many questions, but not the question text unless your own settings expose it.

3. Your content stays yours

You keep all rights to everything you connect or upload. You grant us a limited licence to process it solely to run the service for your workspace: index it, retrieve from it, and generate answers from it. That licence ends when you delete the content or close the workspace. We don't train on it, don't use it for other customers, and don't keep a copy after deletion beyond the backup window in the privacy policy.

4. Our software stays ours

Quire, its interfaces, models, prompts, retrieval systems and documentation are ours. You get a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use them during your subscription. You may not resell the service, reverse-engineer it, use it to build a competing product, or run automated extraction outside the documented API.

5. Acceptable use

Don't use Quire to process content you have no right to, to build profiles of individuals without a lawful basis, to generate material that is unlawful in your jurisdiction or ours, or to attempt to bypass another workspace's access controls. We may suspend an account for a serious violation, and we'll tell you why in writing before or immediately after.

6. What the output is, and isn't

Quire produces cited drafts from your own material. It is not legal, medical, financial or tax advice, and a citation is evidence that a source says something — not proof that the source is correct. You are responsible for review before anything is relied on, sent externally, or filed. Agents that act outside your workspace do so under configuration you control, and you're accountable for those actions the same way you'd be for a person you delegated to.

7. Payment

  • Fees are per seat, billed monthly or annually in advance, in USD or EUR
  • Seat counts are trued up monthly; removed seats prorate as a credit
  • Prices are exclusive of VAT and similar taxes
  • Overdue invoices past 30 days may lead to suspension, after two written notices
  • We won't raise your price mid-term. Renewal increases get 60 days notice

8. Uptime and support

Business and Enterprise plans carry a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment. Below that, you get service credits: 10% of the monthly fee under 99.9%, 25% under 99.0%, 50% under 95.0%. Free and Team plans are provided as-is with commercially reasonable effort and no credits. Scheduled maintenance is announced 72 hours ahead and doesn't count against the number.

9. Cancellation and export

Cancel any time from billing settings. Service continues to the end of the paid period; we don't refund partial months, and we don't make you call anyone. Before or within 30 days after cancellation you can export every notebook, agent definition and trace as Markdown and JSON. After that we delete on the schedule in the privacy policy and send written confirmation.

10. Liability

Neither side is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages. Our aggregate liability is capped at the fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim. These caps don't apply to our breach of confidentiality obligations, our indemnity for third-party IP claims against you, or anything that can't be limited by law.

11. Confidentiality

Each side protects the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care and uses it only for this agreement. Obligations survive for three years after termination, and indefinitely for your customer content.

12. Changes

We may update these terms. Material changes get 30 days notice to workspace admins, and if you don't accept them you may terminate and receive a prorated refund of prepaid fees. We won't apply changes retroactively.

13. Governing law

Israeli law, courts of Tel Aviv, for customers contracting with the Israeli entity. Portuguese law and the courts of Lisbon for customers contracting with the EU entity. Enterprise order forms may specify otherwise.

14. Contact

Legal questions: legal@quire.ai. Everything else: the contact page.
