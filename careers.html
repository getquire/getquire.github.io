Process

Four steps. Twelve days. Paid.

  1. 1 — Intro (30 min). A human, not a form. You ask most of the questions.
  2. 2 — Craft conversation (60 min). Walk through something you built. No whiteboard algorithms.
  3. 3 — Paid work sample (4–6 hours). A real problem from our backlog, paid at your day rate. You keep the code.
  4. 4 — Team day. Meet four people, including someone you'd disagree with. Offer within 48 hours.

We tell you no quickly and with a reason. Roughly 40% of candidates who ask for feedback have used it and reapplied — we've hired three of them.

What we offer

  • Top-of-band salary for your location, reviewed annually with no negotiation required
  • Equity with a 10-year exercise window
  • Four-day week in August. Genuinely, not aspirationally
  • €2,500 yearly learning budget with no approval process
  • Fully covered health cover, and therapy that doesn't need justifying

What we're honest about

We're 31 people. There's no ladder yet, on-call exists, and roughly a fifth of the work is unglamorous parsing. If you want a large org's structure, we don't have it and won't pretend.
