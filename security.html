Commitments

Four things we put in the contract.

We never train on your data

Not foundation models, not a shared index, not "anonymized aggregates". Every model provider we route to operates under a zero-retention agreement, and we'll show you the terms.

Permissions are enforced at query time

Not at index time, not as a post-filter. If your access changed five seconds ago, the next query respects it. Filtering after retrieval leaks through ranking and timing, so we don't.

Your region stays your region

EU, US, Israel or Australia. Data at rest, in flight and in inference stays inside the region you pick, including the model endpoint.

You can see every call

Full trace of every retrieval and model call, exportable to your SIEM. If you can't audit it, you can't approve it.

Architecture

How a query actually flows.

1 · AuthRequest authenticated via SAML/OIDC session or scoped API key. User identity resolved to source-system identities.
2 · RetrievalHybrid search runs against the tenant-isolated index. Permission filters are applied inside the query, not after it.
3 · RerankCandidate spans reranked in our own infrastructure. No customer content leaves the region.
4 · GenerationSelected spans sent to the routed model over TLS 1.3, under zero-retention terms. Nothing else is sent.
5 · GroundingSecond pass verifies each claim against its cited span. Unsupported sentences are removed before you see them.
6 · TraceEverything above is written to an append-only trace log retained for your configured window.

Encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Customer-managed keys via AWS KMS or Azure Key Vault on Enterprise.

Tenant isolation

Separate index namespaces and separate encryption keys per workspace. No shared embedding space, ever.

Deployment

Multi-tenant cloud, single-tenant VPC, or fully in your account with your own model endpoint.
