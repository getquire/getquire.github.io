01 — Library

Index everything. Break nothing.

Quire reads your sources where they live. It does not copy them into a second system of record, and it does not flatten your permissions to make search easier.

  • 42 connectors: Drive, Notion, Slack, Confluence, SharePoint, Gmail, Box, S3, Zendesk, Jira, GitHub, Salesforce, Airtable and more
  • Permission inheritance at the document level, re-checked at query time — not at index time
  • Layout-aware parsing for PDFs: tables stay tables, footnotes stay attached to their claim
  • Incremental sync, so a 40-page edit reindexes in seconds, not hours
  • Native OCR for scans, handwriting and screenshots pasted into tickets

02 — Notebook

An answer you can argue with.

The Notebook is where you actually work: a document that answers back. Ask in the margin, and the answer lands inline with footnotes you can open.

  • Sentence-level citations — page, version, author, timestamp
  • Disagreement detection when two sources give different numbers
  • Explicit refusal when the library has no basis for an answer
  • Follow-ups keep the thread; the notebook is the artifact, not the chat
  • Export to Docs, Notion, Markdown or PDF with citations intact
03 — Agents

The work you do every Monday, done by Sunday night.

An agent is a standing instruction plus a trigger plus an output destination. No graph builder, no node canvas — you write what you want in a sentence and pick when it runs.

Scheduled

"Every Monday 07:00, summarize competitor changes from the tracked sites and post to #market-intel."

Triggered

"When a contract lands in /inbound, redline it against our playbook and flag anything outside our approved positions."

Watching

"Tell me when any document contradicts the security policy page, and quote both sides."

Agents ask before they act

Anything that leaves your workspace — an email, a Jira ticket, a Slack post to a channel you didn't name — waits for approval the first ten times. You promote it to automatic when you trust it.

And they show their work

Every run keeps a trace: what it retrieved, what it skipped, which model answered, what it cost. Replay any run from any point.

Under the hood

Retrieval is the product. The model is a component.

Swapping in a better model gains you a few points. Getting retrieval right is the difference between a demo and a tool people open on Tuesday.

Hybrid retrieval

Dense vectors plus BM25 plus structural filters, fused and reranked. Keyword-exact queries stay exact.

Document graph

Files link to the decisions, people and threads around them, so "the latest version" means something.

Recency weighting

A policy from 2021 loses to the one from March unless you explicitly ask for history.

Grounding check

A second pass verifies every claim against its cited span before you see it. Unsupported sentences get cut.
